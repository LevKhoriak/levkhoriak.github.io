Лев Хоряк

I'm interested in the science of deep learning — how neural networks learn, what they represent, and why they generalize.

I'm an MSc student in Artificial Intelligence at the Faculty of Computer Science, HSE University, and a data scientist at Alfa-Bank, where I build machine learning models in production. Before that, I received a BSc in International Finance. My research interests are in the science of deep learning: learning dynamics, loss landscapes, generalization, representation geometry, and mechanistic interpretability. I'm especially drawn to careful empirical work that tests what theory predicts about real training runs.
